# Psychrometric Chart Guide

Plot air state points on a psychrometric chart by entering temperature and humidity, and instantly read dew point, humidity ratio, specific enthalpy, wet bulb temperature, and specific volume.  
Free features include plotting and comparing up to 9 points (A–I), airflow-based moisture/power/heat conversion, SI and Imperial (US) unit switching, and a low-temperature chart (-60 to 20°C).  
With Pro features, you can also simulate AHU equipment chains (AHU Simulation) and run full-year 8,760-hour HVAC energy simulations using real weather data.
