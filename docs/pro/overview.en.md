# Pro Overview

All core plotter features (points A–I, save/load, compare, unit switching, and low-temperature chart) are free to use.  
For education, HVAC design, and energy analysis workflows, you can upgrade to **Pro** (**Go Professional**).

## 4 Benefits Included in Pro

1. **Ad-free**  
   Removes all banner, app-open, and rewarded video ads (including ad-free saving).
2. **AHU Simulation (AHU Process Design)**  
   Simulate air states step-by-step through a Preheater, Steam Humidifier, Washer, Cooling Coil, and Reheater with real-time chart updates.  
   → See [AHU Process Design](ahu-design.md)
3. **HVAC Simulation (Annual 8,760-Hour Analysis)**  
   Fetch a full year of hourly weather data (8,760 hours) for any city or GPS location, calculate annual/monthly energy use and peak loads, and export all hourly records as CSV.  
   → See [Annual HVAC Simulation](annual-simulation.md)
4. **Also available on the Web**  
   Sign in with Google on Android to unlock the [Web Version](https://psychrometric-chart-bdf76.web.app) from your PC browser using the same Pro entitlement.

!!! tip "Pro Tab Navigation"
    When Pro is active, the top bar switches to a unified **Plotter** / **Simulation** tab bar for instant switching between manual plotting and annual simulation.

## Purchase Options

Open **Menu** → **Go Professional** (or **Settings** → **Go Professional**) to choose a plan:

| Plan | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | Auto-renewing monthly subscription |
| **Yearly** | Auto-renewing annual subscription |
| **Buy Once (Lifetime)** | One-time permanent purchase |
| **3-Day Pass** | 3-day prepaid pass with no auto-renewal—great for short-term projects or evaluation |

*Check the in-app paywall for current pricing in your region.*

## Restore, Manage, and Use on the Web

- **Reinstalling or changing devices**: Tap **Restore Purchases** in **Settings** or on the paywall.
- **Managing subscriptions**: Tap **Manage Subscription** in **Settings** to open Google Play Subscriptions (Lifetime and 3-Day Pass do not auto-renew).
- **Using Pro on the Web**:
  1. Purchase Pro on Android.
  2. In Android **Settings** → **Account**, tap **Sign in with Google**.
  3. Open the [Web Version](https://psychrometric-chart-bdf76.web.app) in your desktop browser and sign in with the **same Google account**.
