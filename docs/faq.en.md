# FAQ & Contact

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Plotting & Chart Basics

??? question "I opened the app and see only a blank chart. How do I start?"
    **Tap anywhere** on the psychrometric chart. A dialog will ask whether to create new points or load saved data—tap **New** to open the entry panel. See the [Getting Started (5-Minute Tutorial)](getting-started.md) for a step-by-step walkthrough.

??? question "Why can't I pair Dew Point and Humidity Ratio together?"
    Dew Point and Humidity Ratio both represent the same water vapor pressure, so combining those two alone does not uniquely determine dry-bulb temperature. Pair either one with Temperature, Relative Humidity, Enthalpy, or Wet Bulb Temperature.

??? question "My plotted points disappeared from the chart!"
    Check **Menu** → **Chart Range**. If **Low Temperature (-60 to 20°C)** is selected (humidity ratio max `0.005 kg/kg(DA)`), warmer or more humid points fall outside the visible range and a badge at the top right displays *"{count} point(s) outside chart range"*. Switch back to **Standard (-10 to 55°C)** to see them again.

??? question "How do I switch to °F, gr/lb, or Btu/lb?"
    Open **Menu** → **Settings** and select **Imperial (US)** under **Unit System**. Existing and saved points convert automatically.

### Save, Ads & Pro Features

??? question "Why does saving ask me to watch a video ad?"
    On the free Android plan, saving a slot requires watching a rewarded video ad. Upgrading to Pro (Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime, or 3-Day Pass) removes all banner, app-open, and rewarded ads.

??? question "I bought Pro on Android, but the Web version says no entitlement was found."
    Please verify both steps:

    1. Open the Android app, go to **Settings** → **Account**, and complete **Sign in with Google** so your purchase syncs to your account.
    2. Sign in to the [Web Version](https://psychrometric-chart-bdf76.web.app) using the **exact same Google account**.

??? question "How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?"
    Sign in to Google Play with the account used for purchase, then tap **Restore Purchases** in **Settings** or on the paywall screen.

### Annual HVAC Simulation (Pro)

??? question "Why doesn't city search match local-language characters?"
    City search uses **Romanized (English) names** (e.g., `Tokyo`, `Munich`, `Zurich`). If a location isn't listed, use **Enter coordinates manually** or **Use Current Location**.

## Contact Us

If you have bug reports, feature requests, or questions, use the built-in contact form inside the app:

1. Open **Menu** → **Settings**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Contact Us**.
3. Choose a category, enter your message (and optional email address for a reply), and submit.

!!! note "Windows Standalone Version"
    On the standalone Windows build, **Contact Us** opens your email client (`ez_games@ymail.ne.jp`).
